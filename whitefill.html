Drag a separate box around each target piece. Whitefill uses pixels inside the boxes and masks the rest, so include the whole piece with a little background. If you use the printed marker for true scale, select the whole marker too. Crop matching areas in each view you want to combine.

2 · Settings

Only pure white pieces are used; other colours and mixed colours are ignored. Cubes and triangles are completed from their own edges. In "whole piece" mode other shapes get a best-guess outline (a smoothed hull of what is visible), so check those. This is a flat extrusion of the photo, not AI 3D reconstruction.
